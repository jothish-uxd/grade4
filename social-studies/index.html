Regions of Canada: Quiz & Picture Guide

21 practice questions about Canada's provinces, its seven landform regions, its natural resources, and how the land shaped the lives of First Peoples. Every question has a picture, and every answer has a map or picture that explains it.

For grown-upsBC's Grade 4 Social Studies course is called First Peoples and European Contact. One of its topics is the landforms and natural resources of Canada, and a big idea is that the search for valuable resources changed the land and its communities. The questions below cover that topic. Teachers sometimes use slightly different names or numbers of regions, so check your child's class notes and use their teacher's names.
